Guest Safety & Policies

House Rules & Booking Policies

Last updated October 2026. Designed to preserve acoustic enjoyment, cleanliness, and a safe atmosphere for every singer squad.

1. General House Rules

  • Zero Vaping / Smoking: All private karaoke suites are strictly non-smoking. Smoke alarms and automated ventilation units are active. Designated outdoor smoking lounges are available on Ground and Rooftop levels.
  • Sound Equipment Care: Our microphones are precision calibrated Shure and Sennheiser condenser units. Do not drop, throw or tap the microphone heads against table surfaces. Guests are responsible for physical equipment damages.
  • Maximum Room Capacity: Room occupancy is strictly limited to posted building safety limits. Our concierge team reserves the right to request group size reductions if a room exceeds capacity.
  • Respectful Conduct: Aggressive behavior or harassment towards staff or neighboring singers will result in immediate session termination without refund.

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Guarantee

We understand plans change! You can reschedule or cancel any reservation directly within your Customer Dashboard:

More Than 24 Hours in Advance

100% full refund of your 20% booking deposit, or free rescheduling to any future available date without fees.

Within 24 Hours of Session

Deposit is converted into a 1-year valid VibeVault credit voucher applicable to any future lounge visit.

3. Age Guidelines & Bar Service

Singers of all ages are warmly welcomed before 8:00 PM every day! After 8:00 PM, all venue patrons must be 18 years or older (or accompanied by an adult guardian). Valid government-issued photo ID is required for alcoholic beverage service.

4. Privacy Policy & Camera Disclosure

For guest privacy, private karaoke rooms DO NOT contain operational audio recording or hidden surveillance cameras. Cameras are strictly located in common corridors, entrance lobbies, and emergency exits for security and safety enforcement.
